# Plentovo panoramic slideshow artwork

Five purpose-designed panoramic scene images replace square category thumbnails in the homepage and collections slideshow. Built-in image generation was used. Every generated scene is 2172 × 724 (3:1), WebP quality 84, with no crop or compositing during encoding. Original generated PNGs were retained during preparation. Existing kitchen and living-room panoramas remain.

## Prompt set and art direction

Shared generation specification: one ultra-wide 3:1 photorealistic Plentovo ecommerce hero photograph; warm ivory, oak, sage and terracotta; natural South African sunlight; premium approachable editorial lifestyle photography. A continuous real environment fills the entire width. No typography, logo, watermark, borders, panels, collage or square photograph pasted onto a solid background. Quieter, softly focused but textured room context on the left 38% accommodates HTML copy. Principal objects on the right 55%, fully visible with safety margins. Camera pulled back for full context and consistent perspective.

- Playtime: complete honey-brown teddy with sage ribbon, wooden stacking rings, blocks and toy house on a cream rug; sunlit playroom with low shelving, woven storage, curtain, wooden floor, plants and reading nook extending across the frame.
- Bedroom: upholstered bed, layered cream linen, sage throw, oak bedside tables, lamps, framed art and rug; continuous room with linen curtains, window, chair and greenery.
- Bathroom: travertine and oak vanity, round mirror, plant and window; complete ivory electric toothbrush, soap dispenser, towels, ceramic basin and toiletry cup on a continuous counter.
- Care: continuous stone vanity, oak drawers, round mirror, linen curtain, plant and stool; complete unbranded amber pump bottle, serum dropper and open ivory cream jar on low stone trays beside a towel. No labels or health claims.
- Outdoor: sunlit South African patio and garden, textured plaster, terracotta planters, greenery, outdoor seating and paving; complete red tricycle with handlebars, seat, pedals and three wheels visible beside a sage ball, inside the frame with safety margins.

## Presentation

All seven slides use the same full-width panoramic desktop composition, a light graduated copy scrim, and controls over the lower image edge. No separate solid-colour text panel or letterboxed square artwork. Mobile displays copy followed by the complete panoramic image. Seven slides rotate every 6.5 seconds, with pause, focus/hover pauses, hidden-tab suspension and reduced-motion support.
